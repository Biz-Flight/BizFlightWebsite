THE BIZFLIGHT APPROACH

Your privacy matters.

This page explains the current static website setup and the information that may be collected when contact features are enabled.

Deployment note: The contact form is not yet connected. Review and update this policy for the actual form provider, analytics tools, and any other data collection before publishing the live site.

Last reviewed: 18 October 2026

1. About this site

BizFlight operates this website to describe software development services and help prospective clients learn how to start a project conversation. The site is designed to use static HTML, CSS, and minimal JavaScript.

2. Information collected

The website currently does not have a live contact submission service or a dedicated backend for collecting enquiries. If a contact provider is enabled, information that you choose to submit may include your name, email address, company name, service interest, engagement preference, and project description.

3. Third-party services

Web fonts may be requested from Google Fonts, subject to Google's applicable practices. The website is planned for GitHub Pages hosting with Cloudflare DNS. Hosting and network service providers may process technical information needed to serve the site. A third-party form provider, such as Google Forms or Typeform, will be disclosed here if enabled.

4. Purpose of enquiry information

When a contact form is activated, information submitted through it is intended to support responding to enquiries, understanding project needs, and discussing potential services. Do not submit sensitive credentials or private system access details through the public form.

5. Retention and requests

Retention details and a verified privacy contact address must be added before the contact form becomes active. Until then, the site does not provide a contact form submission mechanism.

6. Updates

This policy will be revised as website integrations, communication channels, and data handling practices are finalized.
